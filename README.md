## 📋 Reporting Bugs & Crashes

Found an issue or game crash? Please help us fix it by opening an issue:

1. Head over to the [Issues tab](https://github.com/YourUsername/vanilla-encore/issues).
2. Click **New Issue**.
3. Use the **Bug Report** template to provide your modpack version, what happened, and a crash log.

> **Note on Logs:** Please do not paste long crash logs directly into the text box. Upload them to [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs) or [pastebin.com](https://pastebin.com) and link them!
